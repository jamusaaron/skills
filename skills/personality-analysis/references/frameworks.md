# Personality Frameworks

Conventions, scoring scales, and validity notes for each framework used by this skill. Big Five is the scientific backbone; the others are presented as widely used interpretive lenses with known limitations, which the final report must acknowledge.

## Big Five (OCEAN) — primary framework

The five-factor model is the most empirically supported trait taxonomy, and language correlates of its dimensions are the best studied. Report each dimension on a low / below-average / average / above-average / high scale (never fake numeric precision), with 1–2 facets when evidence supports them.

| Dimension | High looks like (in text) | Low looks like (in text) | Key facets to consider |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Openness** | Abstract ideas, novel metaphors, rare vocabulary, aesthetic attention, "what if" framing | Concrete, conventional phrasing, practical focus, established routines valued | imagination, intellectual curiosity, aesthetic sensitivity |
| **Conscientiousness** | Structured organization, planning/time words, achievement language, precise mechanics, follow-through in argument | Loose structure, digressions, casual mechanics, spontaneity valued | orderliness, industriousness, deliberation |
| **Extraversion** | Social words, positive emotion, exclamations, direct audience address, high energy pacing | Reflective interiority, measured pace, few social references, comfort with solitude as topic | sociability, assertiveness, enthusiasm |
| **Agreeableness** | Politeness, we-framing, perspective-taking, softened disagreement, gratitude | Blunt assertion, competitive framing, criticism without cushioning, skepticism of others' motives | compassion, politeness, trust |
| **Neuroticism** | Pervasive negative emotion across topics, anxiety vocabulary, rumination loops, catastrophizing conditionals | Even affect, threat framed as manageable, stable tone under negative topics | anxiety, volatility, self-consciousness |

Validity note for reports: text-based Big Five inference correlates modestly with questionnaire scores in research settings — meaningful, far from deterministic.

## MBTI-style dichotomies — interpretive lens

Popular and intuitive; psychometrically criticized (dichotomizing continuous traits, modest retest stability). Frame results as *preference leanings*, report a leaning + strength (slight / moderate / clear) per axis, and present the 4-letter code as shorthand, not identity. Do not report a code at all if two or more axes are toss-ups — report the axes individually instead.

| Axis | Textual signals toward first pole | Textual signals toward second pole |
|---|---|---|
| **E / I** | Audience-engaging, social content, thinking-out-loud drafting feel, energy from interaction | Reflective, composed interiority, fully formed positions, energy from ideas |
| **S / N** | Concrete detail, sequential accounts, precedent and experience cited, practical focus | Patterns and possibilities, big-picture leaps, analogy-driven, future framing |
| **T / F** | Impersonal criteria, logical consistency prized, critique unsoftened, fairness-as-rules | Values and impact criteria, harmony maintenance, praise and appreciation expressed, fairness-as-care |
| **J / P** | Closure-seeking, plans and verdicts, organized structure, discomfort with loose ends | Options kept open, exploratory structure, qualified conclusions, comfort with ambiguity |

Mapping note: E/I ≈ extraversion; S/N ≈ openness; T/F ≈ (inverse) agreeableness; J/P ≈ conscientiousness. Use Big Five evidence to sanity-check MBTI leanings — divergence between them usually means weak evidence.

## DISC — workplace communication lens

DISC describes observable communication behavior, not deep traits, which makes it comparatively well suited to text evidence. Report a primary style, an optional secondary, and intensity (mild / moderate / pronounced).

| Style | Textual signals | Communication preferences to report |
|---|---|---|
| **D — Dominance** | Direct assertions, imperatives, results language, brevity, challenge framing, low hedging | Bottom line first, options not instructions, speed over polish |
| **I — Influence** | Enthusiasm markers, stories, humor, people references, persuasion over proof | Energy and recognition, verbal processing, dislike of dense detail |
| **S — Steadiness** | Even pacing, we-framing, reassurance, process continuity, softened change talk | Advance notice, sincere appreciation, step-by-step plans |
| **C — Conscientiousness** | Precision, qualifications, data and criteria, structured argument, error-avoidance framing | Detail and accuracy, written follow-ups, time to analyze |

## Enneagram — motivational hypothesis lens

The Enneagram claims to describe core motivations (desires/fears), which are only indirectly visible in text. Treat any typing as the *most speculative* output of this skill: present at most a "most consistent type" plus one alternative, always labeled hypothesis, with the motivational reasoning spelled out. The values-and-motivation marker family (family 9 in `linguistic-markers.md`) is the primary evidence source.

| Type | Core desire / fear | Textual flavor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Reformer | integrity / corruption | should/ought language, precision, corrective stance, suppressed frustration |
| 2 Helper | being loved / being unwanted | other-focus, offering help, warmth, indirect own-needs expression |
| 3 Achiever | being valuable / being worthless | achievement framing, image awareness, efficiency, optimism-as-presentation |
| 4 Individualist | identity / having no significance | authenticity language, aesthetic sensitivity, melancholic depth, set-apart framing |
| 5 Investigator | competence / being overwhelmed | knowledge framing, detachment, precision, boundaries around time/energy |
| 6 Loyalist | security / being without support | contingency thinking, risk scanning, loyalty language, authority ambivalence |
| 7 Enthusiast | satisfaction / being trapped in pain | possibility language, upbeat reframing, plans multiplying, discomfort skipped past |
| 8 Challenger | self-protection / being controlled | strength framing, directness, protective anger, all-in commitments |
| 9 Peacemaker | inner stability / loss and separation | harmonizing, both-sides framing, understatement of own preferences, steady warmth |

## Cross-framework synthesis rules

1. **Big Five anchors everything.** When another framework's result contradicts solid Big Five evidence, the Big Five reading wins and the divergence is reported.
2. **Convergence table required.** The synthesizer lists traits confirmed by 2+ frameworks (high confidence), 1 framework (moderate/low), and contested (report the tension, don't average it away).
3. **Confidence ceiling by evidence quality**: one short formal sample caps every claim at "low confidence" regardless of how loud a marker seems. Multiple samples across registers can support "high confidence" on convergent traits only.
